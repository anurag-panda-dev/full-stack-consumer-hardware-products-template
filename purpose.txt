PURPOSE:
  Full-stack consumer-hardware template — monorepo for connected-hardware products:
  industrial design, electronics, firmware, PLUS companion apps and the device cloud.
  Hardware lives in discipline folders (mechanical/, electronics/, firmware/...), software in
  apps/ + platform/ + kernel/; products/ holds per-line specs pointing into both.
  Empty scaffolding is forbidden: folders fill only as real work arrives.

SHOULD CONTAIN:
  - README.md, AGENTS.md, PACKAGE.md, CONTRIBUTING.md, CHANGELOG.md
  - HARDWARE: products/, mechanical/, electronics/, firmware/, certification/,
  -           manufacturing/, supply-chain/, validation/, packaging/, test-tools/
  - SOFTWARE: apps/, crosscutting/, platform/, kernel/, tests/, datasets/, content/
  - SHARED:   docs/, rfcs/, business/, legal/, labs/, sandbox/, infra/, tooling/, security/
